2AM™ opposes torture, coerced confinement, and physical or psychological abuse in every form, whether carried out by individuals, corporations, or state actors. This page states that position and points readers who need help to independent legal counsel in Nevada.

## Position

- Torture and abuse are non-negotiable. There is no product, contract, or partnership at 2AM™ that permits, requests, or benefits from them.
- Every 2AM™ property, app, and partner brand is expected to comply with U.S. federal law, Nevada state law, and the [UN Convention against Torture](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/convention-against-torture-and-other-cruel-inhuman-or-degrading).
- Reports of abuse involving anyone in the 2AM™ ecosystem are investigated internally and, where warranted, referred to law enforcement.

<Warning>
  If you are in immediate danger, call **911**. This page is a reference, not a substitute for emergency services or legal advice.
</Warning>
