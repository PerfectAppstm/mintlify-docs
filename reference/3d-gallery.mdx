Interactive 3D objects tied to the 2AM™ brandspike. Live models are served from the [Nix6 spatial asset network](https://3dc.nix6.buzz) as `.glb` files and rendered inline with `<model-viewer>`. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

<Tip>
  Click and drag any model to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Auto-rotate is enabled by default.
</Tip>

## Requested but not yet in catalog

The following brandspike slots are reserved. They will render as live 3D once matching `.glb` assets are uploaded to Nix6 (or another host):

- **Neon Skull** — Goth / cyberpunk. For the after-hours side of 2AM™.
- **Crystal Geode** — Luxury / tech. For iBank™, iMoney, and the higher-shelf brands.
- **Chrome Orb** — Abstract / minimal. For UTO™ Core surfaces.
- **Retro CRT TV** — Broadcast tie-in for DopeTV®⁺ and ComTV®⁺.
- **UFO / Antigravity Craft** — Spaceship / antigravity tie-in for spaceship.com and the UFOᐩᐩ line.

<Note>
  To add a live model to any slot: upload the `.glb` to [3dc.nix6.buzz/upload](https://3dc.nix6.buzz/upload), then share the `Download .glb` URL from the model page and it can be wired into this gallery.
</Note>
