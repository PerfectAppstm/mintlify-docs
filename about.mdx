## What is a conglomerate?

A **conglomerate** is a parent organization that holds and coordinates multiple, often unrelated, businesses under a single group. Each business inside a conglomerate keeps its own brand, product, and operations, while sharing capital, leadership, and strategic direction from the parent.

In the 2AM™ context, **Utopia Communities™ Conglomerate** is the parent group. It backs 2AM™ and coordinates a portfolio of apps, product brands, and domains across the wider UTO ecosystem. **Tachyon™ Enterprises** stands alongside it as a co-lead partner. Together they provide the financial, technical, and governance backbone behind 2AM Database\+\+ and every other 2AM™ property listed in the [Database of Links](/reference/database-of-links).
